
### 📌 Return on Investment (ROI) Analysis

Based on current classroom practices, attendance takes approximately 10 minutes per session due to manual roll calls and pronunciation challenges in a multicultural setting.
Assuming each of the 100 faculty conducts 5 classes per day over 20 days:

- Total time lost per month = 1,667 hours
- Estimated cost of lost time = 1,667 × €30 = €50,010/month

However, this proposed NFC-based attendance system has an estimated one-time implementation cost of **€60,000**.

**Key Budget Breakdown:**
- System Design & Development: €25,000
- NFC Device Procurement & Setup: €15,000
- SIS Integration: €10,000
- Training & Rollout Support: €5,000
- Developer Accounts, Monitoring Tools, Cabling, Contingency: €5,000

---

**📊 ROI Calculation:**

- Monthly Savings: €50,010  
- Initial Cost: €60,000  
- ROI = ((50,010 - 60,000) / 60,000) × 100 = -16.65%

This results in an initial **negative ROI of -16.65% in the first month**, indicating that the system would require **at least 2 months** to break even — after which it continues delivering operational cost savings.

---

Despite the upfront cost, this is a **scalable and sustainable digital transformation** that pays back within **~1.2 months**, while also improving data accuracy, administrative reporting, and student satisfaction.
